# Bear case, round 1: ^NSEI, horizon 2w

## Thesis (two sentences)
NIFTY is in a confirmed technical downtrend (death cross, price below all three moving averages, ADX trending at 31.99) that is being driven by three concrete, still-live macro headwinds — a 12%-in-a-month oil spike, a sharp US yield back-up, and reported FII outflows — none of which show signs of reversing. The single most consequential scheduled event inside this ten-day window, the 5-7 October RBI MPC meeting, skews the risk further to the downside because oil-driven inflation gives the committee reason to hold hawkish just as growth data reduces the odds of a dovish surprise.

## Evidence
- Regime is downtrend, "trending" strength, ADX 31.99 with -DI 31.89 dominant over +DI 12.51; price 23,140.5 sits below SMA20 (23,553.3), SMA50 (23,994.46) and SMA200 (24,423.17), with a confirmed death cross (50-day below 200-day) and the 50-day's 1-month slope at -0.88% (source: 01-market.md, technical_report)
- Returns are negative on nearly every window: -0.88% 1w, -4.41% 1m, -3.36% 3m, -8.64% 1y (source: 01-market.md, technical_report)
- Multiple independent outlets confirm a seventh consecutive weekly loss, the longest losing streak since the 2020 Covid crash, and Livemint separately reports it as the biggest weekly drop in 6 years — this is a corroborated regime shift, not a single-source artifact (source: 03-news.md, news #3/#4/#6)
- Three concrete external drivers remain live: WTI crude $92.41 (+12.38% 1m, +33.48% 3m — a direct current-account and inflation hit for a net oil importer), US 10Y yield ~5.184% (+11.15% 1m, +18.57% 3m), and USD/INR 95.82 with the rupee down 0.37% 1m / 0.95% 3m alongside DXY +1.82% 1m (source: 02-fundamentals.md, macro_context)
- The RBI Monetary Policy Committee meets 2026-10-05 to 10-07, squarely inside this 10-day horizon; repo rate is 5.25% with a neutral stance held in August, and oil-driven inflation risk plus strong growth data argue for the committee holding hawkish rather than delivering the dovish surprise that would reverse this move (source: 03-news.md, Scheduled inside horizon)
- Headline tone is one-sided negative: 6 negative vs 1 positive of 15 headlines in the past 7 days, with the negative cluster all repeating the same "longest losing streak since Covid" fact across outlets (source: 04-sentiment.md)
- Nearest support at 23,116.10 and 23,070.15 is already just below spot (23,140.5); a break opens a move toward the lower Bollinger band at 22,876.9 (source: 01-market.md, technical_report)
- S&P 500 is up 0.88% 1m and above its 200-day average over the same period — confirming this is an India/EM-specific repricing, not a global risk-off event that might reverse on its own (source: 02-fundamentals.md / 03-news.md, macro_context)

## Weakest link
The quant direction_forecast itself does not confirm continuation: its probabilities barely move off this symbol's historical base rates (up 52.6% vs base rate 49.4%, an edge of just 3.2 points), it is flagged "no tilt" and "low confidence," and its second-largest driver is "reversion" (+0.079, bullish), not trend. RSI14 at 34.23, stochastic %K/%D at 13.76/17.3, and Bollinger %B at 0.19 all describe a market that is short-term oversold, and realized volatility is only in the 27th percentile of its own history with volume at 0.82x average — this is a calm, low-participation grind lower, not a capitulation, which leaves room for an oversold bounce toward the 50-day SMA before any downtrend reassertion (source: 01-market.md, technical_report).
