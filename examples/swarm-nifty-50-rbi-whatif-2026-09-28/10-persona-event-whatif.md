# Event and special-situation funds on ^NSEI, horizon 1m (what-if: W1)

## What we see
- W1: RBI hikes the repo rate 25 bp to 5.50% on 7 Oct — this is exactly our kind of trade: a scheduled, binary, datable regulatory decision, not macro noise. The prior stance was a hold with a neutral bias (Aug review), so a hike is a genuine hawkish surprise, not a priced-in outcome.
- E1/E2/E3/E5/E6 (baseline macro rout — oil, US-Iran, yields, FPI outflows) set the stage: the index is already down sharply, oversold, and fragile going into the decision. A hawkish surprise on top of that backdrop compounds the negative read rather than getting absorbed calmly.
- Backdrop: WTI up ~35% over 3m, US 10-year at 5.18% and rising, rupee already weakening 0.53% in a month. A domestic hike raises the cost of capital right as global cost-of-capital and terms-of-trade pressures are already building — a genuine negative for equity valuations, even if it offers some rupee support.
- E4 (200-week MA test) and the technical oversold readings (RSI 27.7, %B -0.01) are not inputs for us — we don't trade trend/technicals, we trade the catalyst itself.
- Q2 FY27 earnings season (TCS 8 Oct onward) now opens just one day after the hike, into a higher-rate, higher-input-cost environment — raises the bar for margin commentary and guidance.

## What we do
This is our setup: we position into the 7 Oct decision expecting a possible hawkish surprise given the inflation impulse from oil and the weak rupee, and we react hard on confirmation. A 25bp hike against an already-oversold, falling market reads as a fresh negative catalyst — we add to downside index exposure / cut remaining index-linked longs around the announcement and the Governor's press conference, then reassess quickly once the earnings catalysts (TCS, HCLTech) start printing.
