- E1: Sensex down 1,000+ points, Nifty below 22,850/22,800 on crude, US-Iran tensions, bond yields, FPI outflows. Still the dominant headline driving our initial reaction — we sell into it.
- E2: Rs 5-6 lakh crore wiped out in a session. Viral panic number on retail chat/social media, triggers margin unwinds among leveraged longs.
- E3: ~40% of Nifty 50 down >20% from highs, breadth collapsing. Confirms a broad, not single-stock, rout — feeds capitulation.
- E5: Nifty down ~12% from Sep 2024 peak. "Real bear phase" narrative already circulating before the RBI even moves.
- E4/E6/E7: Secondary color (200-week MA test, macro commentary, NSE listing flop) — reinforce the mood but don't independently move us.
- W1: RBI hikes repo 25bp to 5.50% on 7 Oct, against a backdrop where the last review (August) was a hold with a neutral stance. A hike we weren't bracing for lands as a fresh negative headline exactly while the index is already down, oversold, and jittery — rupee weak, oil up, US yields up. For a headline-driven crowd this reads as "even the RBI is now fighting inflation/currency instead of supporting growth," and it hits right as some of us were starting to nibble the oversold bounce. That nibble gets pulled back fast — this is the kind of single catalyst that can restart the sell-off on its own, independent of whether the broader macro backdrop actually deteriorated.

## What we do
The RBI hike lands as a second wave of bad news on top of an already-panicked tape, and headline-driven retail does what it always does — sells into it, or at minimum stays out of the dip-buy we might otherwise have tried around the 200-week MA / oversold bounce. Expect a fresh, sharper leg of selling and stop-outs concentrated around 7 Oct as the news breaks and social media amplifies "RBI hikes into a crash," before this fades again once the immediate shock passes.
